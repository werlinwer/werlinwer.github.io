FAQ

How do you unlock Snorunt in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Common Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Snorunt hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Snorunt evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Glalie or Froslass at Lv 20. Some forms need an evolution item, bought in the Kecleon Shop or found on bosses.

What is Snorunt's starting move?

Snorunt starts every run with Frost Breath, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Can Snorunt Mega Evolve?

Yes: at its final stage, into Mega Glalie. The Mega Stone drops from the first rift legendary you beat in the run, and the Mega Evolution lasts 20 seconds.

Is Snorunt good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Snorunt is B tier: it beat Mewtwo in 64% of its 87 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
