FAQ

How do you unlock Swablu in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Rare Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Swablu hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Swablu evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Altaria at Lv 20.

What is Swablu's starting move?

Swablu starts every run with Gust, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Can Swablu Mega Evolve?

Yes: at its final stage, into Mega Altaria. The Mega Stone drops from the first rift legendary you beat in the run, and the Mega Evolution lasts 20 seconds.

Is Swablu good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Swablu is C tier: it beat Mewtwo in 53% of its 59 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
