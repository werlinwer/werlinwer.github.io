Mewtwo in Pokémon Survivors

Mewtwo is one of the 23 legendaries in Pokémon Survivors, a Psychic type. The strongest. Devastating psychic damage.

When it shows up

Attacks

  • Phase 1
    • 1 marked strike on the ground near you every 2.4s (28 damage)
    • Ring of 16 projectiles every 3s (26 damage)
    • At range: 5 marked strikes along your path, from where you stand to where you're heading, every 4.5s (30 damage)
    • Up close: Area slam around itself every 4.5s (30 damage)
    • Leaps onto you, a circle marking where it lands, every 6s (28 damage), if you keep away
  • Phase 2 (under 70% HP)
    • 1 marked strike on the ground near you every 2.4s (28 damage)
    • Teleports and lands on a marked circle, every 8s (28 damage)
    • Ring of 16 projectiles every 3.4s (26 damage)
    • At range: 5 marked strikes along your path, from where you stand to where you're heading, every 4.5s (30 damage)
    • Up close: Area slam around itself every 4.5s (30 damage)
    • Volley of 3 projectiles aimed at you, with aim lines before it fires, every 4s (26 damage), under 50% HP
    • Ultimate Telekinesis, every 18s: Ring of 22 projectiles closing on you, with one way out, every 18s (26 damage); Volley of 5 projectiles aimed at you, with aim lines before it fires, every 18s (26 damage)
  • Phase 3 (under 40% HP)
    • 1 marked strike on the ground near you every 2.4s (28 damage)
    • Teleports and lands on a marked circle, every 7.5s (28 damage)
    • Ring of 16 projectiles every 3.2s (26 damage)
    • At range: 5 marked strikes along your path, from where you stand to where you're heading, every 4.5s (30 damage)
    • Leaps onto you, a circle marking where it lands, every 6s (28 damage), if you keep away
    • Leaps onto you, a circle marking where it lands, every 3.8s (28 damage), on Hard and Nightmare
    • Volley of 5 projectiles aimed at you, with aim lines before it fires, every 2.6s (26 damage), on Hard and Nightmare
    • Ring of 12 projectiles every 4.5s (24 damage), on Nightmare only
    • Ultimate Psystrike, every 18s: 5 parallel lances across the screen, the gaps between them safe, every 18s (26 damage); 5 parallel lances across the screen, the gaps between them safe, every 18s (26 damage); A blast over everything (34 damage), except in the marked shelters

Legend Mode

  • Beat it 5 times as a boss, in any mode, and it becomes playable in Legend Mode (its own ranking, a tougher horde, awakenings at Lv 12 and 28).
  • Innate (always on): Pressure
  • Starts with Telekinesis Psychic

★ Beating a shiny one on the catching win, or after, unlocks its shiny.

FAQ

How do you catch Mewtwo in Pokémon Survivors?

Beat it 5 times as a boss, in any mode, and it becomes playable in Legend Mode (its own ranking, a tougher horde, awakenings at Lv 12 and 28). Beating a shiny one on the catching win, or after, unlocks its shiny.

Where does Mewtwo show up?

Boss at 15:00.
