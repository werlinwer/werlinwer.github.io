Raikou in Pokémon Survivors

Raikou is one of the 23 legendaries in Pokémon Survivors, a Electric type. Swift lightning. The fastest of the beasts.

When it shows up

  • Boss at 7:00 in runs that draw the Johto beasts trio (1 in 3)

Attacks

  • Phase 1
    • 1 marked strike on the ground near you every 2.4s (18 damage)
    • At range: 3 marked strikes along your path, from where you stand to where you're heading, every 3s (18 damage)
    • Up close: Leaves a harmful trail for 1.6s (10 damage)
    • Up close: Area slam around itself every 3.8s (20 damage)
    • Leaps onto you, a circle marking where it lands, every 6.5s (20 damage), if you keep away
    • Ring of 10 projectiles every 4.4s (16 damage), on Nightmare only
  • Phase 2 (under 66% HP)
    • 1 marked strike on the ground near you every 2.4s (18 damage)
    • Charges down a marked lane, 3 in a row, every 7s (18 damage)
    • At range: 3 marked strikes along your path, from where you stand to where you're heading, every 3.2s (18 damage)
    • Up close: Leaves a harmful trail for 1.6s (10 damage)
    • At range: 5 marked strikes on the ground near you every 3.2s (16 damage), under 50% HP
    • Ultimate Discharge, every 18s: Area slam around itself every 18s (20 damage); Ring of 14 projectiles every 18s (16 damage)
  • Phase 3 (under 33% HP)
    • 1 marked strike on the ground near you every 2.4s (18 damage)
    • Charges down a marked lane, 2 in a row, every 8s (18 damage)
    • At range: 3 marked strikes along your path, from where you stand to where you're heading, every 3s (18 damage)
    • Up close: Leaves a harmful trail for 1.6s (10 damage)
    • Leaps onto you, a circle marking where it lands, every 6.5s (20 damage), if you keep away
    • Ring of 10 projectiles every 4.4s (16 damage), on Nightmare only
    • Ultimate Thunderclap, every 18s: 4 parallel lances across the screen, the gaps between them safe, every 18s (18 damage); 4 parallel lances across the screen, the gaps between them safe, every 18s (18 damage); 4 parallel lances across the screen, the gaps between them safe, every 18s (18 damage); Charges down a marked lane, every 18s (20 damage)

Legend Mode

  • Beat it 3 times as a boss, in any mode, and it becomes playable in Legend Mode (its own ranking, a tougher horde, awakenings at Lv 12 and 28).
  • Innate (always on): Inner Focus
  • Starts with Thunderclap Electric

★ Beating a shiny one on the catching win, or after, unlocks its shiny.

FAQ

How do you catch Raikou in Pokémon Survivors?

Beat it 3 times as a boss, in any mode, and it becomes playable in Legend Mode (its own ranking, a tougher horde, awakenings at Lv 12 and 28). Beating a shiny one on the catching win, or after, unlocks its shiny.

Where does Raikou show up?

Boss at 7:00 in runs that draw the Johto beasts trio (1 in 3).
