FAQ

How do you unlock Starly in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Common Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Starly hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Starly evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Staravia at Lv 12 and into Staraptor at Lv 28.

What is Starly's starting move?

Starly starts every run with Brave Bird, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Can Starly Mega Evolve?

Yes: at its final stage, into Mega Staraptor. The Mega Stone drops from the first rift legendary you beat in the run, and the Mega Evolution lasts 20 seconds.

Is Starly good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Starly is D tier: it beat Mewtwo in 23% of its 131 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
