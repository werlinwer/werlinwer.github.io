FAQ

How do you unlock Larvitar in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Ultra Rare Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Larvitar hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Larvitar evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Pupitar at Lv 12 and into Tyranitar at Lv 28.

What is Larvitar's starting move?

Larvitar starts every run with Sand Tomb, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Can Larvitar Mega Evolve?

Yes: at its final stage, into Mega Tyranitar. The Mega Stone drops from the first rift legendary you beat in the run, and the Mega Evolution lasts 20 seconds.

Is Larvitar good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Larvitar is S tier: it beat Mewtwo in 91% of its 58 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
