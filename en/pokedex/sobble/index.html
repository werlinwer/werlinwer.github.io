FAQ

How do you unlock Sobble in Pokémon Survivors?

Sobble is a starter: it's unlocked from your very first run, nothing needed.

How does Sobble evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Drizzile at Lv 12 and into Inteleon at Lv 28.

What is Sobble's starting move?

Sobble starts every run with Snipe Shot, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Sobble good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Sobble is D tier: it beat Mewtwo in 46% of its 148 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
