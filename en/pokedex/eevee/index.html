FAQ

How do you unlock Eevee in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Common Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Eevee hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Eevee evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Vaporeon, Jolteon, Flareon, Espeon, Umbreon, Leafeon, Glaceon or Sylveon at Lv 20. Some forms need an evolution item, bought in the Kecleon Shop or found on bosses.

What is Eevee's starting move?

Eevee starts every run with Swift, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Eevee good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Eevee is D tier: it beat Mewtwo in 43% of its 262 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
