FAQ

How do you unlock Abra in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Rare Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Abra hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Abra evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Kadabra at Lv 12 and into Alakazam at Lv 28.

What is Abra's starting move?

Abra starts every run with Future Sight, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Can Abra Mega Evolve?

Yes: at its final stage, into Mega Alakazam. The Mega Stone drops from the first rift legendary you beat in the run, and the Mega Evolution lasts 20 seconds.

Is Abra good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Abra is C tier: it beat Mewtwo in 65% of its 105 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
