FAQ

How do you unlock Piplup in Pokémon Survivors?

Piplup is a starter: it's unlocked from your very first run, nothing needed.

How does Piplup evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Prinplup at Lv 12 and into Empoleon at Lv 28.

What is Piplup's starting move?

Piplup starts every run with Drill Peck, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Piplup good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Piplup is C tier: it beat Mewtwo in 58% of its 193 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
