FAQ

How do you unlock Azurill in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Common Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Azurill hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Azurill evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Marill at Lv 12 and into Azumarill at Lv 28.

What is Azurill's starting move?

Azurill starts every run with Play Rough, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Azurill good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Azurill is C tier: it beat Mewtwo in 53% of its 58 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
