FAQ

How do you unlock Cyndaquil in Pokémon Survivors?

Cyndaquil is a starter: it's unlocked from your very first run, nothing needed.

How does Cyndaquil evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Quilava at Lv 12 and into Typhlosion at Lv 28.

What is Cyndaquil's starting move?

Cyndaquil starts every run with Eruption, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Cyndaquil good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Cyndaquil is D tier: it beat Mewtwo in 46% of its 420 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
