FAQ

How do you unlock Charcadet in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Rare Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Charcadet hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Charcadet evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Armarouge or Ceruledge at Lv 20. Some forms need an evolution item, bought in the Kecleon Shop or found on bosses.

What is Charcadet's starting move?

Charcadet starts every run with Bitter Blade, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Charcadet good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Charcadet is S tier: it beat Mewtwo in 86% of its 105 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
