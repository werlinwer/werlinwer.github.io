FAQ

How do you unlock Fuecoco in Pokémon Survivors?

Fuecoco is a starter: it's unlocked from your very first run, nothing needed.

How does Fuecoco evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Crocalor at Lv 12 and into Skeledirge at Lv 28.

What is Fuecoco's starting move?

Fuecoco starts every run with Torch Song, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Fuecoco good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Fuecoco is B tier: it beat Mewtwo in 65% of its 185 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
