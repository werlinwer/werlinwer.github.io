FAQ

How do you unlock Dreepy in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Ultra Rare Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Dreepy hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Dreepy evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Drakloak at Lv 12 and into Dragapult at Lv 28.

What is Dreepy's starting move?

Dreepy starts every run with Dragon Darts, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Dreepy good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Dreepy is B tier: it beat Mewtwo in 66% of its 100 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
