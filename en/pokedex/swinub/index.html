FAQ

How do you unlock Swinub in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Common Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Swinub hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Swinub evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Piloswine at Lv 12 and into Mamoswine at Lv 28.

What is Swinub's starting move?

Swinub starts every run with Avalanche, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Swinub good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Swinub is A tier: it beat Mewtwo in 78% of its 67 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
