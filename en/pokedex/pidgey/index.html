FAQ

How do you unlock Pidgey in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Common Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Pidgey hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Pidgey evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Pidgeotto at Lv 12 and into Pidgeot at Lv 28.

What is Pidgey's starting move?

Pidgey starts every run with Aerial Ace, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Can Pidgey Mega Evolve?

Yes: at its final stage, into Mega Pidgeot. The Mega Stone drops from the first rift legendary you beat in the run, and the Mega Evolution lasts 20 seconds.

Is Pidgey good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Pidgey is C tier: it beat Mewtwo in 41% of its 100 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
