FAQ

How do you unlock Fennekin in Pokémon Survivors?

Fennekin is a starter: it's unlocked from your very first run, nothing needed.

How does Fennekin evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Braixen at Lv 12 and into Delphox at Lv 28.

What is Fennekin's starting move?

Fennekin starts every run with Psybeam, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Can Fennekin Mega Evolve?

Yes: at its final stage, into Mega Delphox. The Mega Stone drops from the first rift legendary you beat in the run, and the Mega Evolution lasts 20 seconds.

Is Fennekin good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Fennekin is D tier: it beat Mewtwo in 45% of its 247 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
