FAQ

How do you unlock Oshawott in Pokémon Survivors?

Oshawott is a starter: it's unlocked from your very first run, nothing needed.

How does Oshawott evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Dewott at Lv 12 and into Samurott at Lv 28.

What is Oshawott's starting move?

Oshawott starts every run with Razor Shell, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Oshawott good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Oshawott is B tier: it beat Mewtwo in 68% of its 136 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
