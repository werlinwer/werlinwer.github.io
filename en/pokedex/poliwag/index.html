FAQ

How do you unlock Poliwag in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Common Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Poliwag hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Poliwag evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Poliwhirl at Lv 12 and into Poliwrath or Politoed at Lv 28. Some forms need an evolution item, bought in the Kecleon Shop or found on bosses.

What is Poliwag's starting move?

Poliwag starts every run with Bubble Beam, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Poliwag good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Poliwag is A tier: it beat Mewtwo in 70% of its 43 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
