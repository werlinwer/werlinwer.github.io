FAQ

How do you unlock Sandygast in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Haunted Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Sandygast hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Sandygast evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Palossand at Lv 20.

What is Sandygast's starting move?

Sandygast starts every run with Bulldoze, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Sandygast good in Pokémon Survivors?

Sandygast doesn’t have enough runs yet to get a tier on the tier list. It’s updated over time.
