FAQ

How do you unlock Charmander in Pokémon Survivors?

Charmander is a starter: it's unlocked from your very first run, nothing needed.

How does Charmander evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Charmeleon at Lv 12 and into Charizard at Lv 28.

What is Charmander's starting move?

Charmander starts every run with Ember, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Can Charmander Mega Evolve?

Yes: at its final stage, into Mega Charizard X or Mega Charizard Y. The Mega Stone drops from the first rift legendary you beat in the run, and the Mega Evolution lasts 20 seconds.

Is Charmander good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Charmander is D tier: it beat Mewtwo in 44% of its 1,920 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
