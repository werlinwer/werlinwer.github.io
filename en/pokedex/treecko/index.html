FAQ

How do you unlock Treecko in Pokémon Survivors?

Treecko is a starter: it's unlocked from your very first run, nothing needed.

How does Treecko evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Grovyle at Lv 12 and into Sceptile at Lv 28.

What is Treecko's starting move?

Treecko starts every run with Leaf Blade, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Can Treecko Mega Evolve?

Yes: at its final stage, into Mega Sceptile. The Mega Stone drops from the first rift legendary you beat in the run, and the Mega Evolution lasts 20 seconds.

Is Treecko good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Treecko is D tier: it beat Mewtwo in 47% of its 421 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
