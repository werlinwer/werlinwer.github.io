FAQ

How do you unlock Porygon in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Kecleon Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Porygon hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Porygon evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Porygon2 at Lv 12 and into Porygon-Z at Lv 28. Some forms need an evolution item, bought in the Kecleon Shop or found on bosses.

What is Porygon's starting move?

Porygon starts every run with Zap Cannon, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Porygon good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Porygon is S tier: it beat Mewtwo in 69% of its 45 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
