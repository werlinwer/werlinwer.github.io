FAQ

How do you unlock Deino in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Ultra Rare Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Deino hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Deino evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Zweilous at Lv 12 and into Hydreigon at Lv 28.

What is Deino's starting move?

Deino starts every run with Snarl, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Deino good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Deino is A tier: it beat Mewtwo in 91% of its 76 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
