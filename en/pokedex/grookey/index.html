FAQ

How do you unlock Grookey in Pokémon Survivors?

Grookey is a starter: it's unlocked from your very first run, nothing needed.

How does Grookey evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Thwackey at Lv 12 and into Rillaboom at Lv 28.

What is Grookey's starting move?

Grookey starts every run with Drum Beating, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Grookey good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Grookey is A tier: it beat Mewtwo in 68% of its 90 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
