FAQ

How do you unlock Quaxly in Pokémon Survivors?

Quaxly is a starter: it's unlocked from your very first run, nothing needed.

How does Quaxly evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Quaxwell at Lv 12 and into Quaquaval at Lv 28.

What is Quaxly's starting move?

Quaxly starts every run with Aqua Step, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Quaxly good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Quaxly is B tier: it beat Mewtwo in 73% of its 67 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
