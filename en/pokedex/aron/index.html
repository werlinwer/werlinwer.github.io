FAQ

How do you unlock Aron in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Rare Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Aron hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Aron evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Lairon at Lv 12 and into Aggron at Lv 28.

What is Aron's starting move?

Aron starts every run with Heavy Slam, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Can Aron Mega Evolve?

Yes: at its final stage, into Mega Aggron. The Mega Stone drops from the first rift legendary you beat in the run, and the Mega Evolution lasts 20 seconds.

Is Aron good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Aron is B tier: it beat Mewtwo in 78% of its 50 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
