FAQ

How do you unlock Shinx in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Common Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Shinx hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Shinx evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Luxio at Lv 12 and into Luxray at Lv 28.

What is Shinx's starting move?

Shinx starts every run with Wild Charge, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Shinx good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Shinx is C tier: it beat Mewtwo in 46% of its 107 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
