FAQ

How do you unlock Scorbunny in Pokémon Survivors?

Scorbunny is a starter: it's unlocked from your very first run, nothing needed.

How does Scorbunny evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Raboot at Lv 12 and into Cinderace at Lv 28.

What is Scorbunny's starting move?

Scorbunny starts every run with Pyro Ball, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Scorbunny good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Scorbunny is D tier: it beat Mewtwo in 36% of its 316 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
