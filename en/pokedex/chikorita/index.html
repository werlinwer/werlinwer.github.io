FAQ

How do you unlock Chikorita in Pokémon Survivors?

Chikorita is a starter: it's unlocked from your very first run, nothing needed.

How does Chikorita evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Bayleef at Lv 12 and into Meganium at Lv 28.

What is Chikorita's starting move?

Chikorita starts every run with Petal Dance, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Can Chikorita Mega Evolve?

Yes: at its final stage, into Mega Meganium. The Mega Stone drops from the first rift legendary you beat in the run, and the Mega Evolution lasts 20 seconds.

Is Chikorita good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Chikorita is S tier: it beat Mewtwo in 79% of its 151 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
