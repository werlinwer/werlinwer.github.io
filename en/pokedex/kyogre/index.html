Kyogre in Pokémon Survivors

Kyogre is one of the 23 legendaries in Pokémon Survivors, a Water type. Lord of the seas. Brings rain; turns Primal.

When it shows up

Attacks

  • Phase 1
    • 1 marked strike on the ground near you every 2.6s (11 damage)
    • Ring of 12 projectiles every 4.4s (14 damage)
    • A beam out of itself, its line blinking first, every 5.6s (16 damage)
    • 3 marked strikes along your path, from where you stand to where you're heading, every 5s (13 damage)
    • Calls helpers every 12s
    • Ultimate Water Spout, every 26s: A blast over everything (28 damage), except behind a rock
  • Phase 2 (under 68% HP)
    • 1 marked strike on the ground near you every 2.6s (11 damage)
    • Vanishes while its shadow chases you; it stops and it lands there, every 7.5s (22 damage)
    • Volley of 5 projectiles aimed at you, with aim lines before it fires, every 4.8s (14 damage)

    • Calls helpers every 13s
    • Ultimate Surf, every 20s: A blast over everything (30 damage), except on the islands
  • Phase 3 (under 34% HP)
    • 1 marked strike on the ground near you every 2.6s (11 damage)
    • 4 parallel lances across the screen, the gaps between them safe, every 5.2s (23 damage)
    • Ring of 16 projectiles every 4.2s (14 damage)
    • Vanishes while its shadow chases you; it stops and it lands there, every 9s (22 damage)

Legend Mode

  • Beat it 3 times as a boss, in any mode, and it becomes playable in Legend Mode (its own ranking, a tougher horde, awakenings at Lv 12 and 28).
  • Innate (always on): Drizzle
  • Starts with Origin Pulse Water

★ Beating a shiny one on the catching win, or after, unlocks its shiny.

★ Can show up shiny in 1 of 8 rifts: it pays double ₽ plus another Poké Ball.

FAQ

How do you catch Kyogre in Pokémon Survivors?

Beat it 3 times as a boss, in any mode, and it becomes playable in Legend Mode (its own ranking, a tougher horde, awakenings at Lv 12 and 28). In Legend Mode, its second awakening (Lv 28) turns it into Primal Kyogre. Beating a shiny one on the catching win, or after, unlocks its shiny.

Where does Kyogre show up?

In its domain, behind a legendary rift, from 4:00 (optional).
