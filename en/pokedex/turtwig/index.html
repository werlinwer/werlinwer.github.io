FAQ

How do you unlock Turtwig in Pokémon Survivors?

Turtwig is a starter: it's unlocked from your very first run, nothing needed.

How does Turtwig evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Grotle at Lv 12 and into Torterra at Lv 28.

What is Turtwig's starting move?

Turtwig starts every run with Wood Hammer, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Turtwig good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Turtwig is D tier: it beat Mewtwo in 42% of its 224 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
