FAQ

How do you unlock Budew in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Common Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Budew hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Budew evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Roselia at Lv 12 and into Roserade at Lv 28.

What is Budew's starting move?

Budew starts every run with Weather Ball, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Budew good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Budew is B tier: it beat Mewtwo in 65% of its 48 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
