FAQ

How do you unlock Gible in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Ultra Rare Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Gible hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Gible evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Gabite at Lv 12 and into Garchomp at Lv 28.

What is Gible's starting move?

Gible starts every run with Dragon Breath, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Can Gible Mega Evolve?

Yes: at its final stage, into Mega Garchomp or Mega Garchomp Z. The Mega Stone drops from the first rift legendary you beat in the run, and the Mega Evolution lasts 20 seconds.

Is Gible good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Gible is B tier: it beat Mewtwo in 70% of its 94 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
