FAQ

How do you unlock Popplio in Pokémon Survivors?

Popplio is a starter: it's unlocked from your very first run, nothing needed.

How does Popplio evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Brionne at Lv 12 and into Primarina at Lv 28.

What is Popplio's starting move?

Popplio starts every run with Sparkling Aria, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Popplio good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Popplio is A tier: it beat Mewtwo in 64% of its 117 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
