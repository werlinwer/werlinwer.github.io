FAQ

How do you unlock Chimchar in Pokémon Survivors?

Chimchar is a starter: it's unlocked from your very first run, nothing needed.

How does Chimchar evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Monferno at Lv 12 and into Infernape at Lv 28.

What is Chimchar's starting move?

Chimchar starts every run with Mach Punch, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Chimchar good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Chimchar is C tier: it beat Mewtwo in 61% of its 261 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
