FAQ

How do you unlock Smeargle in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Kecleon Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Smeargle hatches, it's yours to play for good.

What is Smeargle's starting move?

Smeargle starts every run with Sketch, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Smeargle good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Smeargle is A tier: it beat Mewtwo in 95% of its 19 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
