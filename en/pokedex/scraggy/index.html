FAQ

How do you unlock Scraggy in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Kecleon Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Scraggy hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Scraggy evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Scrafty at Lv 20.

What is Scraggy's starting move?

Scraggy starts every run with Focus Punch, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Scraggy good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Scraggy is A tier: it beat Mewtwo in 94% of its 16 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
