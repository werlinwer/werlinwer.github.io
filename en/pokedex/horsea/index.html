FAQ

How do you unlock Horsea in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Rare Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Horsea hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Horsea evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Seadra at Lv 12 and into Kingdra at Lv 28.

What is Horsea's starting move?

Horsea starts every run with Brine, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Horsea good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Horsea is B tier: it beat Mewtwo in 65% of its 31 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
