FAQ

How do you unlock Spheal in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Common Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Spheal hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Spheal evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Sealeo at Lv 12 and into Walrein at Lv 28.

What is Spheal's starting move?

Spheal starts every run with Ice Ball, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Spheal good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Spheal is S tier: it beat Mewtwo in 85% of its 73 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
