FAQ

How do you unlock Dratini in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Ultra Rare Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Dratini hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Dratini evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Dragonair at Lv 12 and into Dragonite at Lv 28.

What is Dratini's starting move?

Dratini starts every run with Twister, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Can Dratini Mega Evolve?

Yes: at its final stage, into Mega Dragonite. The Mega Stone drops from the first rift legendary you beat in the run, and the Mega Evolution lasts 20 seconds.

Is Dratini good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Dratini is B tier: it beat Mewtwo in 69% of its 118 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
