FAQ

How do you unlock Togepi in Pokémon Survivors?

By hatching a Common Egg. Bosses, locked chests and rifts sometimes drop eggs, which hatch in the incubator with the steps you walk in runs; once Togepi hatches, it's yours to play for good.

How does Togepi evolve?

Mid-run, as it levels up: into Togetic at Lv 12 and into Togekiss at Lv 28.

What is Togepi's starting move?

Togepi starts every run with Metronome, the family's signature move. The other moves come from level-ups as the line evolves.

Is Togepi good in Pokémon Survivors?

On the tier list, built from players’ runs, Togepi is B tier: it beat Mewtwo in 57% of its 109 runs on Normal over the last 30 days.
