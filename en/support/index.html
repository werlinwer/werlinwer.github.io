Support the project

The game is free and will stay that way, with no ads and nothing for sale that gives power. If you had fun and want to help pay for the servers (accounts, rankings and the Daily Challenge), any amount helps. As a thank-you, donors with an account get cosmetic rewards in the game, nothing that changes a run.

Thank you for your support! ♥

Your redeem code:

In the game, sign in and open the Hall of Fame on the title → I have a code. It works once, for the account that redeems it: it can be a friend's.

Monthly support on Patreon

Want to support every month? On Patreon, your tier follows the amount you keep active. Then link Patreon to your account from the game’s Hall of Fame.

  • Supporter, US$ 5/month: everything donors get, the Supporter role and the exclusive Discord channel, and the Hall of Fame while your support lasts.
  • Gold, US$ 10/month: everything a Supporter gets, the Gold badge and role, a golden card background and wallpaper, and the Hall of Fame for good.
  • Diamond, US$ 20/month: everything Gold gets, the Diamond badge and role, a legendary you captured as your companion (shiny if caught shiny), a monthly vote on a new ultra-rare Pokémon and diamond decorations on the Hall of Fame.

Support on Patreon

Everyone who donated before Patreon is an OG Supporter: they keep everything they have, for good, with their own section on the Hall of Fame.

Who gets the rewards

Optional. With an account's @ (yours or a friend's), the rewards go straight to it. Without one, you get a code to redeem in the game.

What donors get

  • The Supporter title and card frame.
  • A ♥ badge by their @ on the ranking and their profile.
  • A shiny of their choice: any starter or egg family (a family not hatched yet comes with it).
  • A companion that follows them in runs.
  • Their name on the wall below and in the victory thanks.
  • A vote on the next egg Pokémon.
  • Gold and Diamond are monthly now, on Patreon (see below): one-time donations don’t add up to Gold.

All cosmetic: nothing changes a run or the ranking. Rewards need an account in the game (signing in with Google is free).
