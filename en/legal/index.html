Legal, privacy and credits

Unofficial fan game

Pokémon Survivors is an unofficial fan game made by fans for fans by Pedro Barretto. It is free to play; the optional gems sell cosmetic items only and help pay for the servers and development.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed, sponsored or approved by Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, GAME FREAK inc., Creatures Inc. or Spike Chunsoft.

Free, no ads

The game is free and will stay that way: no ads, and nothing that changes a run or the ranking costs money. The only possible purchase is optional gems, for looks (see below).

Poké Dollars (₽) exist only inside the game and can't be bought with real money.

If you want, you can donate on the Support the project page. Donations are voluntary and, with an account, only bring a cosmetic thank-you (a title, a card frame, a badge, a shiny of your choice, a companion), nothing that changes a run or the ranking.

Gems

Gems are a virtual item of the game: they buy cosmetic items only (such as hats, trails and titles), which show in runs, co-op and the Arena, never anything that changes a match or the ranking. They have no cash value, can't be exchanged for money or moved to another account, and stay tied to your game account.

You get gems by playing (the day’s first run of 5 minutes or more, five days of play in the same week, and the Arena, up to a daily limit), by supporting on Patreon or by buying a pack. Each account buys up to R$ 250 or US$ 50 of packs a day, and players under 18 need a parent’s or guardian’s permission to buy. Packs are paid on Stripe (card, Apple Pay and Google Pay) or AbacatePay (Pix), and your payment details go straight to them: the game never sees your card.

Right of withdrawal: within 7 days of buying a pack, you can ask for a refund of its gems you haven't spent yet (Brazilian Consumer Protection Code, art. 49). Email pedro@wavyia.com.br with the purchase reference. Gems already spent and the cosmetic items bought with them are not refundable.

A chargeback or payment dispute removes that pack's gems from the account, even spent ones. The balance can go negative, and while it is, nothing can be bought with gems.

Privacy

Playing as a guest is the default and needs no sign-up: the game has no cookies or ads and never asks for personal data such as your name or email.

Everything the game remembers lives in your browser's localStorage, on your device. As a guest, none of it is sent to any server, except the random id in ph_ps, which goes with the usage statistics (below):

ps:save
Poké Dollars, the upgrades bought in the Kecleon Shop, the in-game Pokédex (species seen, how many you defeated and which you caught), the eggs in the incubator (eggs), the Pokémon hatched (hatched), mission progress, achievements, records (like Pokémon defeated and your longest survival) and the title you picked.
ps:settings
Settings: music and effects volume, sound on or off, damage numbers and screen shake.
ps:lang
Your language (Portuguese, English or Spanish).
ps:muted
Only if you played an older version: the mute flag from before Settings existed. The game only reads it and no longer writes it.
ps:account
Only if you sign in: which account is signed in on this device (its id, your name and your username) and the last version of your progress it synced.
ps:runs
Only with an account: run results that have not reached the ranking yet (for example, while offline). They leave as soon as the server gets them.
ph_ps
A random id for this device, which PostHog uses to count runs and errors (below). It says nothing about who you are.

Accounts are optional and keep your progress in the cloud so you can continue on another device. Sign-in is with Google, handled by Clerk (clerk.com), which gets your name, email and picture from Google and uses its own cookies to keep you signed in. The game never sees your password.

With an account, a copy of ps:save is kept on our server, tied to your account id. It is sent when you sign in, when you open the game and at the end of every run. Our server keeps the account id, your progress, when it was created and last used and what the ranking needs (below), not your email.

With an account, every run also goes to the ranking: the Pokémon, the difficulty, the time survived, the level, how many enemies you defeated, whether you won and the game version, with when the run started and ended, tied to your account id. Guest runs never reach the ranking. The replay of the run goes along (its seed and the inputs you gave, nothing personal), which the server may play again to check the result. It is deleted once checked, or as soon as the server decides not to check the run.

Your profile is public. When you sign in you pick a display name and a unique username, which goes in your profile link. Anyone, with or without an account, can see on the ranking and on your profile: those two names, your equipped title, your records per difficulty, your Pokédex progress, your recent runs and how many followers you have and how many you follow. Your email and picture never show. Who you follow and who follows you is kept on the server; the profile only shows the counts.

Signing out keeps your progress on this device. To delete your account, your profile, your ranked runs and the progress kept in it, ask through the contact below.

To delete this data, clear the site data in your browser settings.

Sprites, sounds and fonts are served by this site, with no calls to third-party services. The site is hosted on Vercel which, like any web server, may log technical access data (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service.

In the game we use PostHog (posthog.com, with data stored in the United States) to understand how it is played and to fix bugs. It receives a summary of each run (Pokémon, difficulty, moves, items, the minute and level you reached and how it ended), which menu screens you open and, when the game hits an error, the error message and the replay of the run in progress (its seed and your inputs, nothing personal). All of it is tied to the random id in ph_ps and, if you signed in, to your account id and username, never to your email or name. PostHog uses no cookies here and does not follow you across sites.

Donations are paid on AbacatePay's pages (abacatepay.com: Pix and Brazilian cards) or Stripe's (stripe.com: cards from other countries, Apple Pay and Google Pay). Your payment details go straight to them, and the game never sees your card. They show us the amount and, as they would to anyone receiving a payment, details of who paid (such as name and email), which we use for nothing but recording the donation. If you give an @ (or redeem the donation's code), it's tied to that game account so the rewards reach it; the amount is never shown publicly, only the supporter badge, which you can hide.
