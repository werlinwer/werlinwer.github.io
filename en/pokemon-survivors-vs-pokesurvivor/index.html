Are Pokémon Survivors and PokéSurvivor the same game?

No. They are two free Vampire Survivors-style Pokémon fan games with almost the same name, made by different people. Pokémon Survivors (pokemonsurvivors.com, this site) is made by Pedro Barretto in Brazil. PokéSurvivor (pokesurvivor.com) is made by JPTER78.

Both came out in October 2026, run in the browser and are free, so search engines and AI chats sometimes mix them up. Here is what sets them apart.

Play Pokémon Survivors if you…

  • want a run with a beginning, middle and end: 15 minutes up to Mewtwo, then the endless mode against Arceus;
  • like theorycrafting: moves that fork into two paths at Lv 4 and Lv 7, Abilities, items, duo moves and Mega Evolution;
  • want to play as the legendaries: catch all 23 by beating them as bosses and play them in Legend Mode;
  • want to play on your phone held upright, one-handed;
  • want a ranking where the top runs are checked by the server;
  • want open source under the MIT license, to study and reuse.

Play PokéSurvivor if you…

  • want to collect: 966 Pokémon and a gacha per region;
  • would rather pick the attacking move yourself;
  • play in Italian or Simplified Chinese;
  • want the bigger community and more YouTube videos.

FAQ

Are Pokémon Survivors and PokéSurvivor the same game?

No. They are two different free fan games by different people, released the same month with almost the same name. Pokémon Survivors is at pokemonsurvivors.com and made by Pedro Barretto. PokéSurvivor is at pokesurvivor.com and made by JPTER78.

I searched for "Pokémon Survivors" and got PokéSurvivor. Did I get the name wrong?

No. Pokémon Survivors, with "Survivors" in the plural, is this game, at pokemonsurvivors.com. Search engines and AI chats sometimes mix the two up.

Which one is better?

It depends on what you want. PokéSurvivor has more Pokémon (966) and a gacha to collect them. Pokémon Survivors has 15-minute runs with legendary bosses, playable legendaries, Mega Evolution, moves that fork into paths and a server-checked ranking. Both are free, so you can play both.

Are both free and official?

Both are free, with no ads, and run in the browser: Pokémon Survivors only sells optional gems, for looks, and PokéSurvivor has no purchases. Neither is official: they are fan games with no ties to Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, GAME FREAK or Creatures.

Can I play Pokémon Survivors with friends?

Yes. Online co-op brings up to 4 players into the same run, each with their own Pokémon. Create a room and invite friends by link, code or friends list. It needs an account.
